WELCOME TO PSG FC

A Gambian football journey

Founded in 2018 with a squad of twenty players, PSG FC began in local tournaments. The team built its experience step by step, taking on Nawettan competition and working through the qualifying rounds.

After several attempts, PSG qualified for the Third Division and won the championship, earning promotion to the Second Division. Two seasons of hard work followed. The club then won the Second Division and earned promotion to the First Division, where it continues to compete.
